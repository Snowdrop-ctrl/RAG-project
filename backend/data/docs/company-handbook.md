# Acme Corp Handbook

## Leave policy
Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused leave may be carried over, up to a maximum of 5 days.

## Working hours
Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible.

## Equipment
Every engineer gets a laptop refresh every 3 years and a 500 USD annual budget
for peripherals such as keyboards, monitors and headphones.
